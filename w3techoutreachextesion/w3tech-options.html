Google Sheet

Every lead found is copied to the Hunt Leads tab of your W3Tech Google Sheet, and updated when you mark it sent. Use the web-app URL and the DEMO_API_KEY of the Sheet's Apps Script (sheets/DemoApi.gs — deploy its latest version).

Which leads to message

Points: no website 40 · only a social page 35 · local business 10 · phone 5 · mobile (WhatsApp) 5 · W3Tech template for the trade 7. A business with no website and a mobile number scores 60–67.

WhatsApp message

Placeholders: {name} {city} {in_city} {pitch} (“noticed you don't have a website yet.” etc.) {demo_link} {category}. You can still edit each message before sending.

Speed

A pause between searches and a daily cap keep Bing from blocking you. If Bing shows a CAPTCHA the hunt pauses by itself.
